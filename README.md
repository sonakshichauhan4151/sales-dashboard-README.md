# Sales Performance Dashboard

Question: Which regions, categories and products drive sales, and how do sales change over time?
Tools: Excel | Data: Superstore Sales (Kaggle), 9,800 rows

## Summary
Sales grew 50% from 2015 to 2018 and peak every September to December. West and East bring 61% of sales. I recommend building stock before September and lifting the South and Central regions.

## Findings
1. Sales grew from $480K (2015) to $722K (2018).
2. September to December brings 52% of yearly sales.
3. West and East bring 61% of sales, and California and New York bring 33%.

Note: the data has Sales only, no Profit, so findings are about revenue.
